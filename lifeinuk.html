Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every question, mock test and video quiz is free, with no sign-up and no adverts in the tests.

Are these the real test questions?

No. They are exam-style practice questions written from the official handbook. The real test questions aren’t published. Practising every chapter is the best way to be ready for whatever comes up.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress, mock scores and wrong answers are saved on your device. Use the same phone or browser to carry on where you left off.

Can I use it on my phone like an app?

Yes. Open this site on your phone, then choose “Add to Home Screen” from the browser menu. It opens straight to your practice.

Who is it for?

Anyone preparing for the Life in the UK Test for settlement (indefinite leave to remain) or British citizenship.
