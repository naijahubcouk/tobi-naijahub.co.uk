Giveaway Terms & Conditions

Auntie Tobi — Your UK Guide for Nigerians

Last updated: September 2026

These terms apply to all Auntie Tobi giveaways unless otherwise stated in a specific promotion.

1 The Promoter

Giveaways are organised and promoted by Auntie Tobi.

Some giveaways may be run in partnership with a third-party business, brand or organisation that provides or sponsors the prize. Where a prize sponsor is involved, their identity will be stated in the relevant giveaway announcement.

Questions about an Auntie Tobi giveaway should be directed to Auntie Tobi through our official contact channels.

2 Eligibility

Unless a particular giveaway states otherwise:

  • Entrants must be aged 18 or over.
  • Entrants must be resident in the United Kingdom.
  • Entrants must have a genuine social-media account where social-media entry is required.
  • Entrants must complete all mandatory entry requirements before the stated closing date and time.
  • Entrants must be able to receive or redeem the advertised prize.

Employees, contractors or anyone directly involved in administering a particular giveaway, together with members of their immediate household, may not enter that giveaway.

3 Free Entry

✅ Unless expressly stated otherwise, Auntie Tobi giveaways are free to enter and no purchase is necessary.

Entrants will not be required to pay Auntie Tobi simply for the opportunity to enter a free prize draw.

4 Giveaway Period

Each giveaway will have an advertised opening date (where applicable), closing date and time, and winner-selection or announcement period. Entries received after the stated closing time will not be included.

Closing dates will not normally be extended or changed after a giveaway begins except where exceptional circumstances make a change necessary. Any material change will be communicated as clearly as reasonably possible.

5 How to Enter

Entry requirements will be stated in each giveaway announcement. These may include:

  • Following specified social-media accounts
  • Liking the giveaway post
  • Commenting on the post
  • Tagging another genuine social-media account
  • Sharing the giveaway
  • Answering a question
  • Completing an online entry form
  • Completing another free entry action clearly described in the promotion

6 Number of Entries

Unless a particular giveaway states otherwise, each person may submit one qualifying entry.

Creating multiple accounts, using false identities, automated entries, bots or other methods intended to obtain an unfair advantage may result in disqualification.

7 The Prize

The specific prize and its approximate value will be stated in the giveaway announcement. Unless the promotion expressly states otherwise:

  • Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash
  • Prizes cannot normally be transferred or sold
  • Prizes are subject to availability
  • No alternative prize is guaranteed simply because a winner would prefer something different

8 Prize Sponsors

Some Auntie Tobi giveaways are sponsored by Nigerian-owned businesses or other partner organisations. Where applicable, the sponsor may be responsible for supplying, delivering or redeeming the prize. Auntie Tobi will identify the prize sponsor in the relevant giveaway announcement where appropriate.

9 Winner Selection

Unless the giveaway is expressly described as a judged competition, winners will be selected randomly from all valid eligible entries.

Winner selection will not be influenced by number of followers, personal relationships, account size, or any factor not included in the published entry rules.

10 Winner Verification

Before confirming a winner, Auntie Tobi may verify that the selected entrant meets the eligibility requirements, completed all required entry steps, entered before the deadline, and has not used fake or duplicate entries.

11 Contacting Winners

Winners will be contacted through one of Auntie Tobi's official channels. Unless a different period is stated, winners should respond within 7 days of the first notification. If a winner does not respond, the prize may be treated as unclaimed and another eligible winner may be selected.

12 Winner Announcements

Auntie Tobi may announce that a winner has been selected through the original giveaway post, Instagram Stories, our website or app, or another appropriate Auntie Tobi channel. We will aim to protect winners' privacy and avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.

13–14 Claiming & Delivery

Winners must follow the reasonable instructions provided for claiming their prize. Any important redemption deadlines or restrictions will be stated in the promotion or communicated to the winner where appropriate.

Whether delivery, postage, travel or other additional costs are included will depend on the individual prize. Any significant cost the winner is expected to pay will be disclosed in the relevant giveaway information.

15 Prize Availability

Auntie Tobi intends to award the prize advertised in each giveaway. The advertised prize will not normally be withdrawn simply because fewer people entered than expected.

16 Disqualification

Auntie Tobi may disqualify entries where there is reasonable evidence of fraud, fake accounts, automated or bot entries, duplicate entries, false information, or breach of these Terms & Conditions. Disqualification decisions will not be made arbitrarily.

17 Fake Accounts & Scams

⚠️ Auntie Tobi will never ask you to pay a fee simply to claim a giveaway prize.

Do not provide your password, Instagram login details, PIN, online banking password, or other sensitive security information. Always check that a winner message comes from the genuine Auntie Tobi account.

18 Personal Information & Privacy

Information collected in connection with a giveaway may be used to administer the promotion, verify eligibility, contact winners, deliver the prize, and prevent fraud.

Entering a giveaway does not automatically subscribe an entrant to Auntie Tobi's marketing communications.

19 Publicity

Winning an Auntie Tobi giveaway does not automatically require someone to appear in promotional content. We may invite a winner to share a photo or feedback — participation will be voluntary unless clearly disclosed before entry.

20 Social-Media Platforms

Auntie Tobi giveaways promoted on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or another social-media platform are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by that platform. Questions should be directed to Auntie Tobi, not the platform.

21–22 Fair Administration & Individual Conditions

Auntie Tobi aims to run giveaways fairly, transparently and consistently. Individual giveaway announcements may include additional conditions relating to eligibility, prize, delivery or entry method. Where a giveaway-specific condition differs from these general terms, the giveaway-specific condition applies to that promotion.

23 Acceptance of These Terms

By entering an Auntie Tobi giveaway, you confirm that you agree to these general Giveaway Terms & Conditions and any additional conditions stated in the relevant giveaway announcement.

Please read the giveaway post carefully before entering.

💚 Giveaway Safety Reminder

Auntie Tobi giveaways are about supporting our community and helping people discover great businesses and opportunities.

We will never ask you to pay a fee simply because you have won a prize. If something does not look right, contact Auntie Tobi through our official channels before responding.
