Our privacy promise

School Tracker handles emails about your children, so we take that seriously. Here’s exactly what we do — in plain English.

The short version

  • We never ask for your email password or access to your inbox.
  • We only see the emails you choose to forward or paste in.
  • Only the people you invite can see your family’s updates.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete any email at any time, or ask us to delete everything.

Who we are

School Tracker is run by Auntie Tobi (auntietobi.co.uk) in the UK. For anything about your data, email info@auntietobi.co.uk.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and first name, so you can sign in with a one-time code.
  • Your family: your children’s first names and schools (so we can match emails to the right child), and the names and email addresses of family members you invite.
  • School emails you send us: the text of each email you forward or paste in, and the summary, to-dos and dates we create from it.
  • Your settings: which email updates you’d like, and who ticked what off.

We don’t need — and don’t ask for — dates of birth, photos, addresses or your children’s own contact details.

How we use it

  • To read your school emails and show you a summary, to-dos and dates for each child.
  • To share your family’s board with the people you invite — only for the children you choose.
  • To send the emails you ask for: sign-in codes, invitations, and your morning and Sunday updates.

That’s it. We don’t use your information for marketing, and we don’t build profiles of you or your children.

Who helps us run it

A few trusted services help run School Tracker. They only process your information to provide the service to you:

  • Netlify — hosts the app and stores your family’s information securely.
  • Resend — receives the school emails you forward, and sends our emails to you.
  • An AI service — each email you send us is passed securely to an AI model just to read it and create your summary, to-dos and dates.

We never sell or share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • School emails stay until you delete them, or until you ask us to close your account.
  • Finished to-dos and past dates are tidied away automatically after about four months.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your family’s information within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any email (and its to-dos and dates) in the app at any time.
  • Turn the morning and Sunday emails on or off in Family → Email updates.
  • The family owner can remove a family member at any time, and they lose access straight away.
  • Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@auntietobi.co.uk and we’ll help.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

School Tracker is for parents and carers. Children don’t use it and don’t have accounts. We only keep their first names and schools, and what’s in the school emails you choose to send us.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping it safe

Everything is sent over secure (HTTPS) connections. Sign-in uses one-time codes, so there are no passwords to steal. Each family’s forwarding address is private and hard to guess — keep it to yourself, like a phone number.
