Questions

Good to know

Who can join the Business Club?

The Business Club is for Nigerian businesses in the UK. If you run any other kind of business, you can use Business Studio — it’s free to start.

How long does membership last?

12 months. It’s £50, paid once a year — about £4.17 a month.

Is Business Studio included?

Yes. You get the full Business Studio — every tool including the advanced ones, 20 daily AI credits, all the Growth Lessons and the full Templates & Tools library.

When will my Spotlight and features happen?

You’ll get at least 1 dedicated Spotlight or collaboration post within your membership year. Discovery features and reposts are scheduled through the year based on campaigns, business categories and content suitability, so how often they happen can vary.

Do you guarantee views or sales?

No. Our goal is to help expose your business to more potential customers and support your growth. We do not guarantee views, enquiries, bookings or sales.
