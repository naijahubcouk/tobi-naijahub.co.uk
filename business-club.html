Verification Opportunity

Business Club members can apply to go through the Auntie Tobi Business Verification process. Businesses that successfully complete the required checks can receive an Auntie Tobi Verified badge on their directory profile.

Membership does not automatically make a business Verified. The badge means the business has completed Auntie Tobi's stated verification checks. It is not a guarantee of the quality of its products or services.
