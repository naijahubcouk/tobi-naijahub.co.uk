How It Works

1

Tell Us About Your Business

Share your business name, what you offer, social details, proposed prize and value, and where you operate.

2

We Review Your Proposal

We review every request to make sure the business and prize are suitable for the Auntie Tobi community. Not every proposal will automatically be accepted.

3

We Agree the Giveaway

Prize, dates, eligibility, delivery, campaign content and sponsor visibility are all agreed before the giveaway goes live.

4

Auntie Tobi Creates the Campaign

We create content designed to make people discover → engage → explore → remember your business.

5

We Promote the Giveaway

The giveaway goes live across agreed Auntie Tobi channels. You may also be asked to share it with your own audience.

6

A Winner Is Selected Fairly

Eligible entries go into a fair random selection process. The selected entrant is verified before being confirmed as winner.

7

The Winner Receives Their Prize

Either Auntie Tobi connects the winner with your business, or your business fulfils the prize directly — as agreed.

What Makes a Good Giveaway Prize?

  • Easy to understand
  • Something people genuinely want
  • Relevant to your ideal customer
  • Easy for the winner to redeem
  • Available within the UK or stated location
  • Valuable enough to create genuine interest

💡 Clear prizes make stronger campaigns. "Win £50 to spend on Nigerian groceries" beats "Win something from our shop."

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to sponsor a giveaway?

Sponsorship options vary depending on the campaign, prize and visibility required. Submit your interest and we'll discuss the most appropriate option for your business.

Do I have to provide the prize?

Usually yes. The sponsor normally provides the advertised prize. Alternative partnership structures can be discussed before the campaign begins.

Can my business sponsor shopping credit?

Yes — for example, £50 to spend with your business. The redemption method and any conditions must be agreed before the campaign launches.

Can I choose the winner?

For random prize draws, winners are selected fairly from eligible entries. For judged competitions, the judging criteria will be agreed and disclosed separately.

Can more than one business sponsor a giveaway?

Yes — for larger campaigns we may work with multiple businesses to create bundles, seasonal or collaborative giveaways.

Can you guarantee sales or entries?

No. Sponsorship provides an opportunity for visibility, discovery, engagement and potential customer acquisition. Campaign performance can vary depending on prize, timing and audience interest.

Can I sponsor more than one giveaway?

Absolutely. Businesses interested in recurring or longer-term partnerships can contact us about ongoing sponsorship opportunities.
