Auntie Tobi Group Advertising Policy
Last updated: 25 August 2026
By joining and making payment for a Group Advertising Campaign, you confirm that you have read and accepted this policy.
Section 1
Purpose of Group Advertising

Auntie Tobi Group Advertising is designed primarily to provide business exposure, brand awareness and discovery. The campaign aims to introduce participating businesses to more people through Auntie Tobi's platform and paid social media advertising.

Advertising results are not guaranteed.

Auntie Tobi does not promise or guarantee a specific number of views, reach or impressions, new followers, likes, comments or shares, website visits or clicks, enquiries, leads or bookings, customers, orders or sales, revenue or return on investment, or any specific advertising outcome.

Results can vary depending on factors including the business category, creative content, audience, location, advertising platform, competition and customer interest.

Participation should be viewed primarily as an exposure and brand-awareness opportunity rather than a guarantee of sales or customers.
Section 2
How the Group Campaign Works

A standard campaign brings together 10 businesses from different business categories within the same city or campaign area. Each participating business contributes £10.

10 businesses × £10 = £100 shared advertising budget.

The £100 contributed by participating businesses is used towards the paid social media advertising campaign.

Auntie Tobi determines the campaign creative, carousel structure, advertising setup, targeting, placement and business slide order based on what we believe will support the overall campaign.

Section 3
When a Campaign Starts

A campaign will normally begin once the required number of suitable participating businesses has been secured and payment made. Auntie Tobi does not guarantee a specific launch date unless one has been confirmed separately.

Showing interest secures your spot. When all 10 spaces are filled you are required to make payment within 6 hours or we will look for a replacement.
Section 4
What Participating Businesses Receive

Subject to the individual campaign details, participation may include:

  • A dedicated business slide within the Group Advertising carousel
  • Business name and relevant information displayed
  • Instagram/social media account tagging where possible
  • Inclusion in the campaign caption or supporting content
  • Participation in the shared paid advertising campaign
  • Approximately 10 days of promotion where stated for that campaign
  • Available campaign performance information after the campaign
  • Addition to the Auntie Tobi Business Directory where applicable
  • Opportunity to complete the Auntie Tobi business verification process
Directory verification is not automatic. A business must satisfy Auntie Tobi's verification requirements before it can be displayed as a Verified Business.
Section 5
Business Information and Content

Participating businesses are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information, including their:

  • Business name and category
  • Location/service area
  • Instagram username
  • Business description or short caption
  • Images and contact details
  • Prices, discounts or promotional offers
  • Website or booking information

Businesses must have the necessary rights or permission to provide any photographs, logos, graphics or other content submitted to Auntie Tobi. Auntie Tobi may edit, shorten or format submitted copy to maintain consistency across the campaign without intentionally changing its meaning.

Section 6
Offers and Campaign Codes

A participating business may choose to provide a special offer, discount or promotion for the campaign. Where a campaign code such as Auntie Tobi is used, the participating business is responsible for clearly stating and honouring the offer and any applicable conditions during the agreed promotional period.

Businesses should keep their own records of enquiries or transactions associated with campaign codes. Auntie Tobi does not guarantee that any campaign code will be used or generate sales.

Section 7
Advertising Budget and Duration

Where a campaign is advertised as having a £100 shared advertising budget, the contributions collected from the 10 participating businesses are allocated towards that campaign's paid advertising.

The advertised campaign duration is an approximate advertising period and may vary slightly because of advertising-platform delivery, review, scheduling or technical issues. Auntie Tobi may adjust campaign targeting, placements or other advertising settings during the campaign where reasonably necessary to improve delivery.

Section 8
Campaign Results

Where available, Auntie Tobi may share campaign-level information such as:

  • Advertising spend
  • Reach and impressions
  • Engagement
  • Profile or link activity
  • Campaign duration
  • Other relevant metrics made available by the advertising platform
These are overall campaign results unless Auntie Tobi specifically states otherwise. A business's inclusion in a carousel does not mean that every person reached by the campaign viewed that individual business's slide.
Section 9
No Guarantee of Results

Participating businesses acknowledge that paid advertising involves uncertainty. Auntie Tobi cannot and does not guarantee that participation will result in views, followers, engagement, enquiries, leads, bookings, customers, orders, sales, revenue or profit.

A campaign performing differently from a business's expectations does not, by itself, mean that the advertising service was not delivered. The primary purpose of the campaign is exposure, awareness and business discovery.
Section 10
Business Categories and Carousel Positions

Auntie Tobi aims to include businesses from different categories within each campaign. Categories may be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to suitability.

Payment does not purchase a particular carousel position.

Auntie Tobi determines slide order and overall campaign structure to create a strong and useful campaign.
Section 11
Advertising Disclosure

Group Advertising is paid promotional content. Auntie Tobi may label campaigns using terms such as AD, Advertisement, Sponsored, Paid Promotion or another appropriate disclosure.

Participation in a Group Advertising Campaign does not mean that Auntie Tobi personally guarantees, certifies or endorses the quality of a business's products or services. Any Auntie Tobi verification status is separate from participation in paid advertising.

Section 12
Cancellations and Refunds

If a business changes its mind before the campaign has been confirmed or production has begun, it should contact Auntie Tobi as soon as possible.

Once a campaign has been confirmed and creative production or advertising has begun, payments may be non-refundable except where required by applicable law or where Auntie Tobi is unable to provide the agreed campaign.

A refund will not normally be provided simply because the campaign did not generate the number of views, followers, enquiries, customers or sales a participating business expected.

Section 13
Right to Refuse or Remove a Business

Auntie Tobi reserves the right to refuse or remove a business from a campaign where there are reasonable concerns about misleading information, unlawful activity, inappropriate content, customer safety, authenticity or suitability for the Auntie Tobi audience.

Where appropriate, a payment may be refunded if removal occurs before the campaign begins.

Section 14
Platform Changes and Technical Issues

Instagram, Meta and other advertising platforms are operated by third parties. Campaign delivery may be affected by platform reviews, outages, account restrictions, advertising-policy decisions, algorithm changes or other matters outside Auntie Tobi's reasonable control.

Where an issue occurs, Auntie Tobi will take reasonable steps to resolve it or adjust the campaign where possible.

Section 15
Agreement

By paying to participate in an Auntie Tobi Group Advertising Campaign, the business confirms that it understands:

The campaign is primarily for exposure, awareness and discovery.

No specific number of views, followers, enquiries, customers or sales is promised or guaranteed.

The business also confirms that the information and content it provides to Auntie Tobi is accurate and that it has authority to use and submit that content.
