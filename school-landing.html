Questions

Good to know

Do I need to change my email address?

No. Your school emails keep arriving in your inbox as normal. School Tracker just gets a copy of the ones you forward to your private address.

Which schools does it work with?

Any school, nursery or club that sends you emails — primary, secondary, independent, after-school clubs. If they email you, it works.

My school uses an app (ParentPay, Arbor, Class Dojo…). Does it work?

Most school apps also send an email — forward those. For messages that only appear in the app, copy the text and use “Paste a school email” — it’s read in exactly the same way.

How does it know which child an email is about?

You tell us each child’s school when you set up. We use that, plus names and year groups in the email. If we get one wrong, tap to change it and we’ll remember that sender next time.

Can my partner use it too?

Yes — invite them from the Family screen. They sign in with their own email, see the children you choose, and get their own morning and Sunday emails. On the Family plan you can invite grandparents and carers too.

Is my children’s information safe?

We never ask for your email password, we only see emails you forward, and only people you invite can see your family’s updates. We never sell or share your data, and you can delete anything at any time. Read the full privacy promise.

Do I need to download an app?

No app store needed. Open School Tracker in your phone’s browser and add it to your home screen — it then opens just like an app.

What if it misreads an email?

The original email is always one tap away, so you can check. You can edit what it’s about, add your own to-dos and dates, or delete anything.
