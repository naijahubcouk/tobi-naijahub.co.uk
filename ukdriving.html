Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Every question, mock test, road sign and video quiz is free, with no sign-up and no adverts in the tests.

Are these the official DVSA questions?

No. They are exam-style practice questions written independently from The Highway Code, Know your traffic signs and NHS first-aid guidance. The official question bank isn’t published, so learning the rules is the best way to be ready.

Does it include hazard perception?

Not yet. You’ll find how part 2 works and how it’s scored, but there are no practice clips yet — GOV.UK has an official practice test you can use for that part.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress, mock scores and wrong answers are saved on your device. Use the same phone or browser to carry on where you left off.

Can I use it on my phone like an app?

Yes. Open this site on your phone, then choose “Add to Home Screen” from the browser menu. It opens straight to your practice.
