Privacy

This page covers the free practice sites lifeinuktest.auntietobi.co.uk and ukdrivingtheorytest.auntietobi.co.uk, run by Auntie Tobi. Last updated 27 November 2026.

No account needed

You don’t need to sign up or give us your name, email or phone number to use the practice tests.

Your progress stays on your device

Your answers, mock test scores, wrong answers to retry, your personal plan and your test date (if you add one) are saved in your browser on your own device. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

If you choose “Save my progress”

This is optional. If you type your email to save your progress, we keep your email address and a copy of your practice progress (the same things listed above) so you can carry on on any phone or computer. We send you a one-time code and link by email to confirm it’s you — no password.

We only use your email to send you your code and link. We never sell or share it.

To delete everything we hold, open the menu, choose My saved progress, then Delete saved progress. It is deleted straight away, on every device. You can also email us to ask.

Statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts are used (for example, how many mock tests are started), so we can improve the sites. It uses cookies and collects things like your browser type, rough location and pages visited — not your name. You can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or a blocker.

Calendar reminders

If you add your test date to your calendar, the reminder file is made on your device and saved to your own calendar app. We don’t see it.
