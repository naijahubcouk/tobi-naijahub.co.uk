Privacy

This page covers the free practice sites lifeinuk.auntietobi.co.uk and ukdriving.auntietobi.co.uk, run by Auntie Tobi. Last updated 26 November 2026.

No account, no personal details

You don’t sign up or give us your name, email or phone number to use the practice tests.

Your progress stays on your device

Your answers, mock test scores, wrong answers to retry and your test date (if you add one) are saved in your browser on your own device. We don’t receive them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts are used (for example, how many mock tests are started), so we can improve the sites. It uses cookies and collects things like your browser type, rough location and pages visited — not your name. You can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or a blocker.

Calendar reminders

If you add your test date to your calendar, the reminder file is made on your device and saved to your own calendar app. We don’t see it.
