Questions
What is an “AI generation”?

Each time you tap Create or Regenerate and get a result, that’s one generation — even when you get several options (like 3 captions). A 30-day plan uses 3. Editing, copying, saving and making a result shorter or more conversational are free. If something goes wrong and you don’t get a result, it isn’t counted. Your allowance resets every night at midnight.

Does it post to Instagram for me?

No — you stay in control. Business Studio writes the content; you copy it and post or send it yourself, wherever your customers are (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp or your website).

Business Club or Pro — which is right for me?

The Business Club is for Nigerian businesses in the UK. Choose it if you want Auntie Tobi to help people discover your business — Spotlight, Featured Business, Marketplace listing and reposts — plus every tool. Choose Pro or Plus if you only want the tools and more generations a day. You can have both.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — in 2 taps: Account → Cancel subscription, or straight from the reminder email. Pro and Plus renew automatically, and we remind you before every payment (3 days before for monthly; 7 days and 1 day before for yearly). If you cancel, you won’t be charged again and you keep your plan until the end of the period you’ve paid for, then go back to the Free plan. Your saved content stays in your library.

Can I switch between Pro and Plus?

Yes — from Account → “Switch plan, manage or cancel”. You can also move between monthly and yearly.

Is my business information private?

Your Business Studio is private to your account — your profile, drafts and library aren’t shown to anyone else. Your price list page is only public if you choose to publish it. To write your content, your request and business profile are sent to our AI provider.

Will the content sound like me?

It uses the tone you choose (warm, professional, fun…) and your own details — products, customers, location and calls to action. You can always edit it before you post.
