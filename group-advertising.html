Auntie Tobi Group Advertising
Affordable advertising. Bigger reach. Together. 💚
Advertising your small business shouldn't have to cost hundreds of pounds. Auntie Tobi Group Advertising brings Nigerian-owned businesses together to share the cost of a larger advertising campaign.

Instead of one business paying £100 for an advertising campaign:
10 businesses contribute £10 each
10 businesses × £10 = £100 advertising budget

100% of the £100 contributed goes towards paid advertising for the group campaign.
Auntie Tobi does not take a campaign fee from the £100.
How does it work?

Each campaign brings together 10 Nigerian businesses from different business categories in one UK city. Each participating business contributes £10. Your business is then featured alongside nine other Nigerian businesses in a dedicated Auntie Tobi Carousel post.

£100 is collected → £100 goes into paid advertising → the post is promoted for 10 days.

The aim: help more people discover Nigerian-owned businesses while making paid advertising more affordable for small businesses.

One business per category

We want each participating business to have its own space to stand out. That's why we aim to feature only one business from each category in each campaign.

If the Makeup Artist space has already been taken, another makeup artist will not be added to that campaign. The same applies to catering, cakes, photography, hair, fashion, logistics and other services.

Your £10 secures your category for that campaign.
Category availability is first come, first served.

What happens after you join?
1. Secure your space
Choose an available city campaign and pay your £10 contribution.
2. Send your business information
You'll be asked to provide:
  • Business name
  • Instagram handle
  • Business category
  • Location/areas served
  • Short description of what you offer
  • Suitable business image, logo or other requested content
3. Auntie Tobi creates the campaign
All 10 businesses are brought together into one Carousel discovery-style post.
4. The post goes live
Participating businesses are tagged. We encourage everyone to share the post with their own audience. The more businesses support the campaign, the greater the opportunity for everyone.
5. Organic exposure comes first
The post is published organically on Auntie Tobi. Paid promotion begins approximately 24 hours after publication. Advertising start times can occasionally vary due to platform review or approval.
6. The paid campaign runs
The combined £100 advertising budget promotes the post for approximately 10 days.
7. You receive a campaign report
Auntie Tobi provides all participating businesses with a report showing how the shared advertising budget was used.

However, as Auntie Tobi Group Advertising develops, we may also introduce dedicated UK-wide campaigns for businesses that genuinely serve customers nationally — fashion brands, beauty products, food with nationwide delivery, online stores, logistics, professional services, travel, gifts and more.

If your business operates nationwide, you can still register your interest for future campaigns.

Why group advertising?
10 × £10 = £100

A small business may not be ready to spend £100 on one advertising campaign. But by advertising together, participating businesses can contribute a relatively small amount while benefiting from a larger combined advertising budget.

You aren't paying £100 by yourself.
You're putting £10 into a £100 group advertising campaign.

And when participating businesses share the campaign with their own audiences, the campaign has an opportunity to benefit from the combined communities of all the businesses involved.

Small contribution. Bigger campaign. Grow together. 💚
Your Instagram page matters 📱
"The advert is the door that brings people into your shop — your Instagram page is the shop itself."
Paid advertising can help bring more people to your business. But what happens after they discover you matters too. Before participating, make sure your Instagram page clearly shows:
  • What your business offers
  • Where you're based and/or areas you serve
  • How customers can order, book or contact you
  • Clear photos or videos of your products/services
  • Recent and relevant content
  • Customer reviews or examples of your work, where available
  • A clear Instagram bio with working website/contact links
Your Instagram page is part of your marketing. Auntie Tobi's role is to help put your business in front of more people. Your page, products, pricing, customer service and overall experience also play an important role in what happens next.

Participating businesses are responsible for ensuring all information provided to Auntie Tobi is accurate and up to date, including business name, products, services, prices, offers, location, delivery areas, contact information, Instagram details, website information, and images or promotional material supplied for the campaign.

Businesses should inform Auntie Tobi if important information changes before the campaign is published. Paid campaigns will be clearly identified as advertising.
